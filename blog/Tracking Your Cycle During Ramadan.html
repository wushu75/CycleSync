Your body doesn't pause for Ramadan. Here's how to track your cycle through the holiest month of the year.

Fasting, prayer, and your menstrual cycle all happening at once. For Muslim women, Ramadan brings a quietly complex intersection — and no period tracker has ever been built for it. Until now.

T
Tony MomohCreator of CycleSync · November 2026

Every Ramadan, millions of Muslim women navigate the same private calculation: which days can I fast? Which days can't I? How many days do I need to make up? And somewhere in that calculation is their menstrual cycle — a schedule they can't control, happening in the middle of the holiest month of the year.

Most women track this in their head. Some use a notebook. A few use a generic period app that knows nothing about Ramadan, nothing about Qada, and nothing about what it means to plan your worship around your body.

CycleSync now has a Ramadan Mode — the first cycle tracker built specifically for the Muslim experience.

"Knowing your cycle during Ramadan isn't just health awareness — it's spiritual preparation. When you know what's coming, you can plan your most powerful nights of worship."

The experience nobody talks about

Your period arrives during Ramadan. Under Islamic law, you don't fast on those days — the ruling is clear and deeply compassionate. But those days need to be made up after Ramadan ends. That's your Qada count.

For many women, keeping an accurate Qada count while also tracking their cycle health, their symptoms, and their energy through the month happens entirely in their memory. It's a quiet burden that most period apps simply ignore.

What Qada means Qada refers to the obligation to make up missed fasting days after Ramadan. For women, days when menstruation makes fasting impermissible are automatically exempt — but must be repaid before the following Ramadan. Keeping an exact count matters.

What Ramadan Mode does

Activate Ramadan Mode in CycleSync and the Islamic calendar overlays your cycle view. At a glance, you can see which days of Ramadan fall during your period, your fertile window, your luteal phase, and your highest-energy days.

Fasting Day Logger. Log each day you fast with a single tap. On days when menstruation makes fasting impermissible, CycleSync automatically notes the day — keeping your record clean without any manual tracking.

Qada Counter. Your missed fasting days accumulate automatically. At the end of Ramadan, you'll know exactly how many days to make up — no mental arithmetic, no notebook, no guessing.

Laylat al-Qadr planning. The last ten nights of Ramadan are the most sacred. CycleSync shows you your cycle phase during those nights — so you can plan your ibadah around your body's natural energy levels, not against them.

Planning your worship around your cycle

Your menstrual cycle affects your energy, focus, and emotional state throughout the month. Understanding this during Ramadan can transform how you approach worship:

Your cycle phases during Ramadan Follicular phase (after period ends): Energy rises. Ideal for intensive Quran study, longer night prayers, memorisation.

Ovulation: Peak energy and focus. Many women find this their most powerful time for ibadah.

Luteal phase: Energy slows. Gentle dhikr, reflection, and lighter worship may feel more natural.

Menstruation: Rest is prescribed. Focus on dua and spiritual reflection rather than formal prayers and fasting.

Your data stays with you — always

CycleSync is built local-first. Everything you log — your fasting days, your Qada count, your cycle data — is stored on your device and never sent anywhere. No servers. No data sharing. No company holding your most personal health information.

This matters especially for faith-related health data. What you record in CycleSync belongs only to you.

Available on Chrome, Edge, and as a web app

CycleSync works wherever you do — as a Chrome extension, on Microsoft Edge, and as a Progressive Web App you can add to your phone's home screen. All free. All private.

Опыт, о котором никто не говорит

Менструация приходит во время Рамадана. Исламский закон ясен: в эти дни не постятся. Но эти дни нужно возместить после Рамадана — это ваш счётчик Када.

Что означает Када Када — обязательство восполнить пропущенные дни поста после Рамадана. Дни менструации автоматически освобождены, но должны быть возмещены до следующего Рамадана.

Что делает режим Рамадан

Включите режим Рамадан в CycleSync, и исламский календарь наложится на ваш вид цикла. С первого взгляда вы увидите, какие дни Рамадана совпадают с менструацией, овуляцией и пиком энергии.

Журнал поста. Отмечайте каждый день поста одним нажатием. В дни менструации CycleSync автоматически фиксирует день, сохраняя точный счётчик Када.

Счётчик Када. Пропущенные дни накапливаются автоматически. В конце Рамадана вы будете знать точное число дней для возмещения.

Планирование Лайлат аль-Кадр. Последние десять ночей Рамадана — самые священные. CycleSync покажет вашу фазу цикла в эти ночи.

Foi & Santé du Cycle

Votre corps ne fait pas de pause pendant le Ramadan. Voici comment suivre votre cycle pendant le mois le plus sacré de l'année.

Jeûne, prière et cycle menstruel — tout en même temps. Pour les femmes musulmanes, le Ramadan apporte une intersection subtilement complexe pour laquelle aucun tracker de cycle n'avait été conçu. Jusqu'à maintenant.

T
Tony MomohCréateur de CycleSync · Janvier 2027

Chaque Ramadan, des millions de femmes musulmanes effectuent le même calcul privé : quels jours puis-je jeûner ? Combien de jours dois-je rattraper ? CycleSync propose désormais un Mode Ramadan — le premier tracker de cycle conçu spécifiquement pour l'expérience musulmane.

"Connaître son cycle pendant le Ramadan, ce n'est pas seulement une question de santé — c'est une préparation spirituelle."

L'expérience dont personne ne parle

Vos règles arrivent pendant le Ramadan. La loi islamique est claire : on ne jeûne pas ces jours-là. Mais ces jours doivent être rattrapés — c'est votre compteur Qada.

Ce qu'est le Qada Le Qada désigne l'obligation de rattraper les jours de jeûne manqués après le Ramadan. Les jours de menstruation sont automatiquement exemptés mais doivent être remboursés avant le prochain Ramadan.

Ce que fait le Mode Ramadan

Activez le Mode Ramadan dans CycleSync et le calendrier islamique se superpose à votre vue du cycle. D'un coup d'œil, vous voyez quels jours du Ramadan coïncident avec vos règles, votre fenêtre fertile et vos jours d'énergie maximale.

Journal de jeûne. Enregistrez chaque jour de jeûne d'un seul toucher. Les jours de menstruation sont notés automatiquement.

Compteur Qada. Vos jours manqués s'accumulent automatiquement. À la fin du Ramadan, vous saurez exactement combien de jours rattraper.

Planification de Laylat al-Qadr. CycleSync affiche votre phase de cycle pendant les dix dernières nuits.

Vos données restent avec vous — toujours

CycleSync est local-first. Tout ce que vous enregistrez reste sur votre appareil. Aucun serveur. Aucun partage de données.

Iman & Kesehatan Siklus

Tubuhmu tidak berhenti di bulan Ramadan. Begini cara melacak siklusmu di bulan paling suci dalam setahun.

Puasa, shalat, dan siklus menstruasi — semuanya sekaligus. Bagi perempuan muslim, Ramadan membawa persimpangan yang kompleks yang belum pernah dirancang oleh aplikasi pelacak siklus manapun. Sampai sekarang.

T
Tony MomohPencipta CycleSync · Januari 2027

Setiap Ramadan, jutaan perempuan muslim melakukan perhitungan yang sama: hari apa saya bisa berpuasa? Berapa hari yang harus saya ganti? CycleSync kini memiliki Mode Ramadan — pelacak siklus pertama yang dirancang khusus untuk pengalaman muslim.

"Mengetahui siklusmu selama Ramadan bukan sekadar kesadaran kesehatan — ini adalah persiapan spiritual."

Pengalaman yang tidak pernah dibicarakan

Menstruasimu datang di bulan Ramadan. Hukum Islam jelas: kamu tidak berpuasa pada hari-hari itu. Tapi hari-hari itu harus diganti setelah Ramadan — itulah penghitung Qadamu.

Apa itu Qada Qada mengacu pada kewajiban mengganti hari-hari puasa yang terlewat setelah Ramadan. Hari-hari menstruasi otomatis dibebaskan tetapi harus dibayar sebelum Ramadan berikutnya.

Apa yang dilakukan Mode Ramadan

Aktifkan Mode Ramadan di CycleSync dan kalender Islam akan ditampilkan bersama tampilan siklusmu. Sekilas, kamu bisa melihat hari Ramadan mana yang bertepatan dengan menstruasi, jendela subur, dan hari-hari energi tertinggimu.

Pencatat Puasa. Catat setiap hari puasa dengan satu ketukan. Hari menstruasi dicatat otomatis.

Penghitung Qada. Hari-hari yang terlewat terakumulasi otomatis. Di akhir Ramadan, kamu tahu persis berapa hari yang harus diganti.

Perencanaan Lailatul Qadar. CycleSync menampilkan fase siklusmu selama sepuluh malam terakhir.

Datamu tetap bersamamu — selalu

CycleSync adalah local-first. Semua yang kamu catat tersimpan di perangkatmu. Tidak ada server. Tidak ada berbagi data.

যে অভিজ্ঞতার কথা কেউ বলে না

রমজান মোড কী করে

CycleSync-এ রমজান মোড চালু করুন এবং ইসলামী ক্যালেন্ডার আপনার চক্র দৃশ্যে যুক্ত হয়। এক নজরে দেখুন রমজানের কোন দিনগুলো আপনার পিরিয়ড, উর্বর সময় এবং সর্বোচ্চ শক্তির দিনের সাথে মিলে।

রোজা লগার। প্রতিটি রোজার দিন এক ট্যাপে নথিভুক্ত করুন। পিরিয়ডের দিনগুলো স্বয়ংক্রিয়ভাবে নোট হয়।

কাযা কাউন্টার। ছেড়ে যাওয়া দিনগুলো স্বয়ংক্রিয়ভাবে জমা হতে থাকে। রমজান শেষে আপনি জানবেন ঠিক কতদিন কাযা করতে হবে।

信仰と周期の健康

ラマダン中も体は動き続けます。一年で最も神聖な月に、生理周期を記録する方法。

断食、礼拝、月経周期——すべてが同時に。ムスリム女性にとってラマダンは複雑な交差点をもたらしますが、それに対応した周期トラッカーはありませんでした。今まで。

T
Tony MomohCycleSync 開発者 · 2027年1月

毎年ラマダンになると、何百万人ものムスリム女性が同じ計算をします。どの日に断食できるか？何日分補うべきか？CycleSyncにはラマダンモードが追加されました——ムスリムの経験のために作られた初めての周期トラッカーです。

"ラマダン中に自分の周期を知ることは、健康意識だけではありません——それは精神的な準備です。"

誰も話さない経験

ラマダン中に生理が来ます。イスラム法は明確です：その日は断食しません。しかしその日はラマダン後に補わなければなりません——これがカダーカウンターです。

カダーとは カダーとはラマダン後に逃した断食日を補う義務のことです。月経中の日は自動的に免除されますが、次のラマダンまでに補う必要があります。

ラマダンモードの機能

CycleSyncでラマダンモードをオンにすると、イスラム暦が周期ビューに重なります。ラマダンのどの日が生理、排卵、エネルギーのピークと重なるかが一目でわかります。

断食ログ。 断食した日をワンタップで記録。生理中の日は自動的に記録されます。

カダーカウンター。 逃した日数は自動的に積算されます。ラマダン終了時に補うべき日数が正確にわかります。

ライラトゥルカドル計画。 最後の10夜のサイクルフェーズが表示されます。

データはあなたのそばに——常に

CycleSyncはローカルファーストです。記録したすべてのデータはあなたのデバイスに保存され、どこにも送られません。サーバーなし。データ共有なし。

Il tuo corpo non si ferma durante il Ramadan. Ecco come tracciare il tuo ciclo durante il mese più sacro dell'anno.

Digiuno, preghiera e ciclo mestruale — tutto allo stesso tempo. Per le donne musulmane, il Ramadan porta un'intersezione sottilmente complessa per cui nessun tracker di ciclo è stato progettato. Fino ad ora.

T
Tony MomohCreatore di CycleSync · Gennaio 2027

Ogni Ramadan, milioni di donne musulmane fanno lo stesso calcolo privato: in quali giorni posso digiunare? Quanti giorni devo recuperare? CycleSync ora ha una Modalità Ramadan — il primo tracker di ciclo creato specificamente per l'esperienza musulmana.

"Conoscere il proprio ciclo durante il Ramadan non è solo consapevolezza della salute — è preparazione spirituale."

L'esperienza di cui nessuno parla

Le mestruazioni arrivano durante il Ramadan. La legge islamica è chiara: quei giorni non si digiuna. Ma quei giorni devono essere recuperati dopo il Ramadan — questo è il tuo contatore Qada.

Cos'è il Qada Il Qada indica l'obbligo di recuperare i giorni di digiuno persi dopo il Ramadan. I giorni di mestruazione sono automaticamente esentati ma devono essere ripagati prima del prossimo Ramadan.

Cosa fa la Modalità Ramadan

Attiva la Modalità Ramadan in CycleSync e il calendario islamico si sovrappone alla tua vista del ciclo. A colpo d'occhio, vedi quali giorni del Ramadan coincidono con le mestruazioni, la finestra fertile e i giorni di massima energia.

Registro del digiuno. Registra ogni giorno di digiuno con un tocco. I giorni di mestruazione vengono annotati automaticamente.

Contatore Qada. I tuoi giorni mancati si accumulano automaticamente. Alla fine del Ramadan, saprai esattamente quanti giorni recuperare.

Pianificazione Laylat al-Qadr. CycleSync mostra la tua fase del ciclo durante le ultime dieci notti.

I tuoi dati rimangono con te — sempre

CycleSync è local-first. Tutto ciò che registri rimane sul tuo dispositivo. Nessun server. Nessuna condivisione dei dati.

没有人谈及的经历

月经在斋月期间到来。伊斯兰法律很清楚：那些天不封斋。但这些天需要在斋月后补上——这就是你的补斋计数器。

什么是补斋 补斋是指在斋月后补上错过封斋日的义务。月经期间的日子自动豁免，但必须在下一个斋月前补上。

斋月模式的功能

在CycleSync中开启斋月模式，伊斯兰历就会叠加到你的周期视图上。一眼就能看出斋月的哪些天与月经、排卵期和精力最旺盛的日子重叠。

封斋日记录。 一键记录每个封斋日。月经期间的日子自动标注。

补斋计数器。 错过的日子自动累积。斋月结束时，你会清楚地知道需要补多少天。

盖德尔之夜计划。 CycleSync在最后十夜显示你的周期阶段。

你的数据始终属于你

CycleSync采用本地优先架构。你记录的一切都存储在你的设备上，从不发送到任何地方。没有服务器。没有数据共享。
