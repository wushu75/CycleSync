Origin Story

I sketched an idea. Then I built it — alone, from scratch, one version at a time.

On 24 March 2025, CycleSync didn't exist. Eighteen months later it runs on Chrome, Edge, and mobile in 14 languages. This is what happened in between.

T
Tony MomohCreator of CycleSync · October 2026

It started the way most things I've built start — with a sketch. Not a business plan, not a pitch deck. Just an idea scribbled down: what if a period tracker lived in your browser, stored nothing on a server, and asked for nothing in return?

I've been in tech since the early 90s. I can read code. I understand branding, UX, product. I've worn every hat at some point. But CycleSync wasn't a calculated product decision. It was curiosity with conviction behind it.

Version 1.0 — simple, basic, a beginning

The first version was bare. A period tracker. That was it. No languages, no themes, no PWA, no Edge version. Just the core idea working in a browser extension — your data on your device, no account, no server.

I tested it myself. Then I sent it to my sisters. When you're building something for women, the people closest to you become your first honest critics. They used it. They told me what felt off. I fixed it and built more.

"What if we made it pink?" That thought came from understanding the people I was building for — not from a trend report or a focus group.

I'm a designer. Colour isn't decoration to me — it's communication. Pink, lavender, rose, cream. These weren't arbitrary choices. They were the result of actually thinking about who opens this extension and what feeling it should give them. Something warm. Something theirs.

The dog walks and the debug sessions

I have a dog. Every day, between builds, I'd take her out. Those walks became part of the process — stepping away from the screen, letting an idea settle, coming back with fresh eyes. Some of the best decisions about CycleSync happened on those walks.

Then back to testing. Reading through the code, hunting for bugs, fixing what broke. No team to hand it off to. No QA department. Just me, the extension, and the question: does this work the way a real person would use it?

Everything, done by one personThe idea. The name. The branding. The UX. The code. The copy. The testing. The translations. The Chrome Store listing. The Edge submission. The PWA. The landing page. The blog. All of it — solo, from 24 March 2025 to today.

From 1.0 to 1.2.2 — what grew with each version

  • 1.0
    The beginning — 24 March 2025Basic period tracker. English only. The idea proven to itself.
  • 1.0.7
    Colour, character, and feelingThe rose and lavender palette takes shape. CycleSync starts to look like something worth using.
  • 1.1.0
    Languages — the world opens upA user in Russia wrote to say privacy mattered to his girlfriend. That message changed everything. Languages followed — Russian, Arabic, Portuguese, German, Spanish, and more.
  • 1.2.0
    Edge, PWA, and getting seriousChrome extension, Microsoft Edge extension, and a mobile PWA. Three platforms, zero app stores, one codebase.
  • 1.2.2
    14 languages · getcyclesync.com · the blogA proper domain. A proper blog. Users in Algeria, India, UAE, Brazil, the US, the UK. The flywheel turning on its own.

The moment it stopped feeling like a side project

There wasn't one single moment. It was cumulative. A stranger in Russia installing it and writing to say it mattered. Algeria appearing in the install data without any effort on my part. chatgpt.com sending traffic because users were recommending CycleSync to each other through an AI assistant. A 26% install conversion rate on the Chrome Store — five times the industry average.

Why local-first was never a compromise

Your period data never leaves your device. There is no CycleSync database of your health records. If we were ever served with a legal request for user data, we would have nothing to hand over — not because we deleted it, but because we never had it. That's not a limitation. That's the product.

Storia delle origini

Ho abbozzato un'idea. Poi l'ho costruita — da solo, da zero, una versione alla volta.

Il 24 marzo 2025 CycleSync non esisteva. Diciotto mesi dopo funziona su Chrome, Edge e mobile in 14 lingue. Ecco cosa è successo nel mezzo.

T
Tony MomohCreatore di CycleSync · Ottobre 2026

È iniziato come iniziano la maggior parte delle cose che ho costruito — con uno schizzo. Non un piano aziendale, non un pitch deck. Solo un'idea scritta: e se un tracker del ciclo vivesse nel tuo browser, non memorizzasse nulla su un server e non chiedesse nulla in cambio?

Sono nel settore tecnologico dai primi anni '90. So leggere il codice. Capisco branding, UX, prodotto. CycleSync non è stata una decisione di prodotto calcolata — era curiosità con la convinzione dietro.

Versione 1.0 — semplice, base, un inizio

La prima versione era minima. Un tracker del ciclo. Tutto qui. L'ho testato io stesso, poi l'ho mandato alle mie sorelle. Quando costruisci qualcosa per le donne, le persone più vicine a te diventano le tue prime critiche oneste.

"E se lo facessimo rosa?" Quel pensiero è venuto dalla comprensione delle persone per cui stavo costruendo — non da un report di tendenze o un gruppo di discussione.
Tutto, fatto da una sola personaL'idea. Il nome. Il branding. L'UX. Il codice. I testi. I test. Le traduzioni. La scheda Chrome Store. La submission Edge. La PWA. La landing page. Il blog. Tutto — da solo, dal 24 marzo 2025 ad oggi.

起源故事

我画了一个想法的草图。然后我把它建造出来 — 独自一人，从零开始，一个版本接一个版本。

2025年3月24日，CycleSync还不存在。十八个月后，它以14种语言在Chrome、Edge和手机上运行。这是中间发生的事情。

T
Tony MomohCycleSync创始人 · 2026年10月

它的开始方式和我建造的大多数东西一样 — 从一个草图开始。不是商业计划，不是路演材料。只是一个写下来的想法：如果一个经期追踪器住在你的浏览器里，不在服务器上存储任何东西，也不索取任何回报，会怎样？

我从90年代初就在科技领域。我能读懂代码。我理解品牌、用户体验、产品。CycleSync不是一个经过计算的产品决策 — 它是充满信念的好奇心。

1.0版本 — 简单、基础、一个开始

第一个版本很简单。一个经期追踪器。就这些。我自己测试了，然后发给了我的姐妹们。当你为女性构建东西时，最亲近的人成为你最初的诚实批评者。

"如果我们把它做成粉色呢？"这个想法来自于理解我为之构建的人 — 而不是趋势报告或焦点小组。
一切，由一个人完成想法。名字。品牌。用户体验。代码。文案。测试。翻译。Chrome商店上架。Edge提交。PWA。落地页。博客。一切 — 独自一人，从2025年3月24日到今天。
