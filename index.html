How to use the mobile app

Using CycleSync on your phone

CycleSync has a free mobile web app (PWA) that works on iPhone and Android. It does not require an App Store or Google Play — just open it in your mobile browser.

🍎 iPhone (iOS)
Requirements: iPhone with iOS 16.4 or later. Use Safari browser — Chrome on iPhone does not support PWA installation.
1

Open Safari on your iPhone

Must be Safari — not Chrome or Firefox on iPhone

2

Go to the app

Visit wushu75.github.io/CycleSync/app/

3

Tap the Share button ⬆️

The box with an arrow at the bottom of Safari

4

Tap "Add to Home Screen"

Scroll down in the share sheet to find it

5

Tap "Add" ✅

CycleSync appears on your home screen like an app

🤖 Android
Requirements: Android 8.0 or later. Use Chrome browser for the best experience and automatic install prompt.
1

Open Chrome on your Android

Chrome gives you the automatic install prompt

2

Go to the app

Visit wushu75.github.io/CycleSync/app/

3

Tap "Install" when prompted

A banner appears automatically after a few seconds

4

Or tap the menu ⋮ → "Add to Home Screen"

If no banner appears, use the Chrome menu manually

5

Tap "Add" ✅

CycleSync appears on your home screen like an app

⚠️ Common mistakes

✗
Using Chrome on iPhone — Chrome on iPhone does not support PWA installation. You must use Safari.
✗
Not adding to home screen — If you just bookmark it the app won't work offline and won't look like an app. You must add it to your home screen.
✗
Old iOS version — PWA installation requires iOS 16.4 or later. Update your iPhone if it's not working.
✗
Opening in Facebook or Instagram browser — Always open in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android) directly, not inside another app.
FAQ

Common questions

Is CycleSync really free?

Yes — CycleSync is completely free. No subscription, no hidden fees, no account required. If you find it useful you can optionally support development with a coffee via Ko-fi.

Is my cycle data private?

100% private. All your data is stored locally in your browser using Chrome's built-in storage. Nothing is sent to any server, no account is required, and your data is never shared or sold to anyone.

Does CycleSync work on Windows?

Yes — CycleSync works on any device that runs Google Chrome, including Windows, Mac and ChromeOS.

Can CycleSync predict my period?

Yes — once you enter your last period date and cycle length, CycleSync automatically predicts your next period and ovulation date. The more you track the more accurate it becomes.

Is CycleSync good for PCOS tracking?

Yes — CycleSync lets you log symptoms daily including mood, energy, flow, cramps and more. This makes it useful for anyone tracking irregular cycles including those with PCOS.
